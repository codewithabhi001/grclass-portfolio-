Entry Survey requirements

To commence the class admission process, the following ship documents must be transmitted to GR Class:

  • ✓ Updated ship survey status & recommendations
  • ✓ Copies of existing class & statutory certificates
  • ✓ Registry papers & main plans (GA, structural)
  • ✓ Trim & stability booklet & thickness reports

SERVICE CATEGORY 04

Compliance & Remote Support

Port State Control & Remote Surveys
Port State Control (PSC)

Recognizing the critical nature of PSC, we collaborate closely with Flag States and ship operators to improve fleets' records. We assist with PSC pre-arrival checklists, sharing detention prevention actions, and compliance with the latest Regional MOUs (Paris MoU, Tokyo MoU).

Technical Advisory Services

We provide expert marine engineering consultancy, operational optimization, and risk mitigation to help vessels operate safely with reduced cost.

Remote Survey Solutions

We execute remote inspections to support operational continuity where onboard access is restricted or unavailable:

  • Occasional surveys between scheduled periods
  • Documentation-based remote approvals
  • Verification of minor deficiencies rectification
  • Interim audits for ISM, ISPS, & MLC

Plans, Manuals & Stability Verification
Plan Approval Process

Before construction, modification, or major repair of a vessel, our technical office verifies the structural plans and layout details:

Key Plans

GA blueprint, Midship, Shell expansion, Hatch covers.

Safety & Systems

Fire control, LSA, fuel/ballast piping layouts.

Manual Approvals & calculations

We review and approve several critical marine manuals, including:

SOPEP / SMPEP BWMP (Ballast) STS Ops Manual Garbage Mgmt Plan Fire Control Plans
Vessel Admission to Class

Application to GR Class is a smooth process. Owners can submit entry requests directly. We will:

  1. 1. Obtain survey and cert history from the losing society.
  2. 2. Review registry documents & stability booklets.
  3. 3. Coordinate vessel attendance to minimize disruption to commercial voyages.
Statutory certificates are issued on behalf of Flag States following completion of applicable technical surveys.
